How to Use the Writing Goal Tracker

The Goal Tracker turns a word count into a target you can see. Set the number of words you want to write, then watch the progress bar fill as you type. It runs in your browser and doesn't send your text to our servers.

  1. Enter your Word Target. The default is 500. Use a number above zero.
  2. Start writing or paste your text. The status line updates live, showing your words, your target, and the percentage complete.
  3. Watch the bar. When it is full, you have reached your goal. If you keep writing, the word count keeps rising, but the percentage stops at 100%.
  4. Copy your work before you leave. This page doesn't save your text or your target, so refreshing or closing it clears everything. For auto-saved documents, use the main editor.

How Words Are Counted

The Goal Tracker counts words the same way as the main WordCounterPro counter. A word starts with a letter or number and can include apostrophes and hyphens, so "don't" and "well-known" each count as one word. Other punctuation ends a word, so a number such as 3.5 or a web address is counted as several words.

Other programs may count differently, so if you are working to a strict limit set by a school, publisher or employer, check how that organization counts.

Choosing a Word Target

  • 250 words: a gentle daily habit, about one short paragraph or two.
  • 500 words: a steady daily goal, or one blog post section.
  • 1,000 words: a serious daily goal for experienced writers.
  • 1,667 words: the daily pace needed to write a 50,000-word novel draft in 30 days.
  • An assignment limit: enter your required length, and use the guides below to split it by section.

Tips

  • Start smaller than you think. A goal you meet every day beats a big one you skip.
  • Set a goal per section. Our guide to structuring academic essays shows how to budget words across an essay.
  • Use it as a limit. If you have a maximum, enter it as the target. A full bar means you have reached it, so check the number in the status line to see whether you have gone over.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the Goal Tracker save my progress?

No. Your text and target are not saved on this page, so copy your work before closing or refreshing it. The main editor can auto-save your documents in your browser.

Does it track characters or pages?

No, this tool tracks words only. The main word counter shows characters, sentences, paragraphs and more.

Why is my count different from another tool?

Tools follow different counting rules, especially for hyphenated words, contractions, numbers and symbols. Check which rule your school or publisher uses.

What happens if I go over my target?

The bar stays full and the percentage stays at 100%, but the word count in the status line keeps rising, so you can see how far over you are.

What if I leave the target empty or set it to zero?

The tracker treats it as a target of 1, so the bar will fill as soon as you write a word. Enter a number above zero for a useful goal.

Is my text uploaded?

No. The count happens in your browser, and this page doesn't send your text to our servers.
