Word and Character Limits: A Quick Reference

Limits vary by platform and change over time, but these are the numbers writers ask about most.

Characters

WhereTypical limit
X (Twitter) post, standard accounts280 characters
Instagram caption2,200 characters
LinkedIn post3,000 characters
YouTube video title100 characters
SMS text message (basic GSM characters)160 characters per message
Page title (SEO)about 50–60 characters before it may be cut off
Meta description (SEO)about 150–160 characters before it may be cut off
Common application essay250–650 words, depending on the prompt

Platforms change these numbers from time to time, so check the current rules before you publish.

Words

As a rough guide, a short blog post runs 600–1,000 words, a standard article 1,000–2,000 words, and a long guide 2,500 or more. A double-spaced page of text holds about 250–300 words, while a single-spaced page holds roughly 500. These are conventions, not rules, so always follow the brief you’ve been given.

Do spaces count?

It depends. Most platform limits count spaces and punctuation. Some academic or translation briefs specify characters without spaces. The Details panel shows both figures, so check which one your limit uses.
