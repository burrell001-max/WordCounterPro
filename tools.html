More tools

Everything below is built into the editor. Choose a tool to open it.

Find & replaceSearch with match case and whole-word options, then replace one or all. Clean textStrip extra spaces, blank lines, line breaks, HTML tags and emoji. Typing speed testMeasure your words per minute and accuracy on a short passage. Writing goalsSet minimum and maximum targets in words, characters, sentences or pages. ActivitySee the words you wrote over the last seven days. SettingsDark mode, fonts, reading and speaking speeds, and the Details panel. Case converterOpen the Case menu for upper, lower, title and sentence case. Proof read & Talk to typeHear your text read aloud, or dictate by voice, in supported browsers. Keyword densityFind your most-used words and two- and three-word phrases.
