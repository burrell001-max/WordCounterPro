Speech Writing: Timing and Word Counts

Writing a speech differs significantly from writing an essay because speeches are meant to be heard, not just read. A crucial element of speech preparation is calculating exact presentation timing based on speaking pace.

The Standard Speaking Pace (150 WPM)

On average, conversational speakers talk at approximately 150 words per minute. This means a standard 5-minute speech requires roughly 750 words. Using built-in speaking time estimators helps ensure you never run over your allotted presentation window.

Practicing and Proofreading Aloud

Reading your draft aloud or using text-to-speech proofreaders allows you to catch tongue-twisters, awkward pauses, and pacing issues before stepping up to the podium.
