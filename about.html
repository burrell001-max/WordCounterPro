About WordCounterPro

WordCounterPro is a free writing tool that shows you the numbers behind your text while you type.

Why it exists

People check word counts constantly: students against essay limits, marketers against character limits, editors against a brief. Most counters do the basic job and stop. We wanted one place that also handles the tasks that come next, such as tidying pasted text, setting a target range, hearing your draft read aloud and checking whether your sentences all sound the same.

Private by design

Counting and analysis run in your browser. Your writing is not uploaded to our servers, and there are no accounts. Saved documents live in your browser’s local storage on your own device, and you can delete them at any time.

What it does not do

WordCounterPro counts, estimates and highlights. It does not check grammar, spelling or plagiarism, because those need outside services that would have to receive your text. Reading level, reading time and Flow Score are estimates, and counts can differ slightly from other tools because there is no single official definition of a word.

How the site is funded

The tool is free to use. To cover hosting and development time, the site may show advertising from Google AdSense. Ads never read your text. See our privacy policy for how advertising cookies work and how to opt out of personalised ads.
