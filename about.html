About WordCounterPro

A free, private word counter and writing toolkit.

WordCounterPro started with a simple frustration: most word counters are cluttered, slow, or want you to sign up before they'll count a paragraph. We built a cleaner one that opens instantly and works the way writers expect.

What you can do here

Count words, characters, sentences, and paragraphs as you type. See your reading level, estimated reading and speaking time, and your most-used keywords. Check sentence rhythm with Flow Score, set word-count goals, dictate with voice typing, and listen to your draft read aloud to catch awkward phrasing. Separate tools handle case conversion and text cleanup, and our writing guides cover word limits, readability, and structure.

Privacy by design

Your text is processed in your browser and isn't sent to our servers. There are no accounts and no sign-ups. Read the details in our Privacy Policy.

Who's behind it

WordCounterPro is built and maintained by Kent Williams, an independent developer and writer. Kent designs, codes and updates every tool on the site, and writes the guides in the blog.

How the site is funded

The site is free to use and supported by advertising. Ads never change how the tools count or analyze your text.
