Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

WordCounterPro ("we," "us") provides free writing tools at https://www.YOURDOMAIN.com. This policy explains what information is and isn't collected when you use the site.

Your text stays on your device

Text you type, paste, or upload into our tools is processed in your browser. We do not send it to our servers and cannot read it. If auto-save is on, your documents and settings are stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Information we may collect

Like most websites, our hosting provider and analytics or advertising partners may automatically receive technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, and pages visited. We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect names or addresses. If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message, and we use them only to reply.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads on this site. Google and other third-party vendors use cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, to serve ads based on your previous visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.
  • To learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads or non-essential cookies are used, and can change that choice at any time through the consent notice on the site.

Third-party services

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and scripts from the cdnjs content delivery network. When your browser requests these files, those providers receive your IP address and standard request data. Their own privacy policies apply to that information.

Voice features

The "Talk to Type" feature uses your browser's built-in speech recognition. Depending on your browser, audio may be processed by the browser vendor (for example, Google in Chrome) under that vendor's privacy policy. We do not receive or store your audio. The "Read Aloud" feature uses your browser's built-in speech synthesis.

Children

This site is a general-audience writing tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, use the opt-out links above, or clear local storage to remove saved documents. Blocking cookies will not stop the tools from working.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
