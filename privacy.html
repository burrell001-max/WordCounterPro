Privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you use WordCounterPro (“we”, “us”). We’ve tried to keep it plain and accurate.

Your text

The text you type, paste or upload is processed in your browser. We don’t send it to our servers and we can’t see it. Find and replace, cleaning, counting, Flow Score, PDF creation and printing all happen on your device.

Data stored in your browser

If auto-save is on, WordCounterPro stores your documents, settings, goal, and a seven-day tally of words written in your browser’s local storage. This data stays on your device. You can delete it with the Delete and Clear buttons, by turning auto-save off, or by clearing your browser’s site data.

Voice features

Proof read uses your browser’s text-to-speech. Talk to type uses your browser’s speech recognition. In many browsers, such as Chrome and Edge, speech recognition sends your audio to the browser maker’s servers for processing, under that company’s privacy policy. The microphone is used only while Talk to type is switched on, and you can block access in your browser’s site settings.

Advertising and cookies

We may use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet. Ads do not have access to the text in the editor.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ personalised advertising at aboutads.info.
  • Learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent before using advertising cookies, and you can change your choice at any time.

Fonts

This site loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser requests them from Google, which receives your IP address as part of that request.

Messages you send us

If you contact us, we receive the details you choose to include, such as your name, email address and message, and we use them only to respond. Sending a bug report with “Include my browser and screen details” ticked adds your browser type and screen size to the message.

Your rights

Because we hold no account data and don’t store your text, there is generally nothing for us to export or delete. If you’re in a region with data protection rights (such as the EU, UK or California) and believe we hold information about you, contact us at email and we’ll respond.

Children

WordCounterPro is a general-audience tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.

Contact

Questions about this policy: email, or use the contact page.
