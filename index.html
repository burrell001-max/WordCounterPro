    A free word counter and character counter for writers

    Paste or type your text above and WordCounterPro shows your word count, character count, sentences and paragraphs immediately, with no sign-up.

    Many writing tasks come with a number attached: a 500-word essay, a 280-character post, a 160-character meta description, a 1,200-word article for a client. This page is built for checking those numbers quickly and then going further. Keep several documents open at once, set a minimum and maximum target, see how long your piece takes to read or say out loud, and find the repeated words you lean on too often.

    Everything happens in your browser. Open the page, write, and your documents stay on your device until you delete them.

    How it works

    Writing tools

    Find & replace supports match case and whole-word searches. Clean text removes extra spaces, blank lines, line breaks, HTML tags and other clutter that tends to arrive when you paste from a web page or PDF. Case changes the capitalisation of a selection or of the whole text. Upload opens a .txt file, and Download saves your work as a .txt or PDF. Print sends just your text to the printer.

    Voice tools

    Proof read speaks your text out loud, which is a reliable way to catch clumsy phrasing and missing words. Talk to type turns your speech into text. Both use features built into your browser, so availability varies by browser and device.

    Details panel

    The panel on the right tracks words, characters, sentences, paragraphs, reading level, reading time, speaking time and handwriting time. Open “More” for average sentence length in words and characters, publisher word count, unique words, syllables, longest and shortest sentences and more. In Settings you choose which rows to show and adjust the reading, speaking and handwriting speeds.

    Goals and activity

    Set a goal in words, characters, sentences or pages, with a minimum, a maximum or both. The progress bar tells you how far you have to go, when you’re inside your range and when you’ve gone over. Activity charts the words you added over the last seven days.

    Flow Score and keyword density

    Flow Score sorts every sentence into one of six length bands, from 1–5 words up to 36 or more, and colours each sentence so you can see your rhythm at a glance. Keyword density lists your ten most-used words or two- and three-word phrases, which helps you spot overused terms.

    How words and characters are counted

    Words are sequences of letters or digits, including inside-word apostrophes and hyphens. Characters include spaces and line breaks; “characters, no spaces” is in the Details panel. Sentences end at a full stop, question mark or exclamation mark followed by a space or the end of the text. Paragraphs are separated by line breaks. Because there is no single official definition, other tools may differ by a few words, especially with abbreviations, URLs and symbols.

    Frequently asked questions

    How does WordCounterPro count words?

    A word is any run of letters or digits, and it may include apostrophes and hyphens inside it. So “don’t” and “well-known” each count as one word. Numbers such as 2026 count as words too.

    Is my text uploaded anywhere?

    No. Counting, Flow Score, find and replace, PDF creation and every other tool run in your browser. Your text is not sent to our servers. The one exception is the optional Talk to Type feature, which uses your browser’s own speech service. See the privacy policy for details.

    Where are my documents saved?

    With auto-save on, your documents are stored in your browser’s local storage on this device. Clearing your browser data or switching browsers or devices means they won’t be there. Download a .txt or PDF copy of anything important.

    How is reading time worked out?

    Reading time divides your word count by a reading speed of 238 words per minute, a commonly used average for silent reading. Speaking time uses 150 words per minute and handwriting time uses 20. You can change all three in Settings.

    What is the Flow Score?

    Flow Score looks at how much your sentence lengths vary across six length bands, and penalises long runs of similar-length sentences. A high score means a varied rhythm. It is a rough guide to readability, not a grade.

    What is the publisher word count?

    Some publishers and editors estimate length from characters instead of counting words, using characters with spaces divided by six. It can differ from the normal word count, so it is shown as a separate figure in the Details panel.

    Does it check grammar or plagiarism?

    No. Those checks need outside services that would have to receive your text. WordCounterPro focuses on counting, timing, keyword density and sentence rhythm.
