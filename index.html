    What is WordCounterPro?

    WordCounterPro is a free writing tool that updates your word, character, sentence and paragraph counts while you type. Paste a draft in, trim it to a limit, and watch the numbers move.

    Hit a target

    Set a word goal for an essay, article or post. The bar under the editor fills as you write, and Activity shows how many words you added each day this week.

    Spot repeated words

    Keyword density lists your most-used words and phrases with their share of the text, so you can catch overused terms before a reader does.

    Your text stays with you

    Nothing you type is sent to a server. Auto-save keeps a copy in this browser only, and you can turn it off or clear it at any time. Reading level is an estimate based on the Flesch–Kincaid grade formula, so treat it as a guide.

    Blog

    Cutting a draft to a word limit

    Start with the Details panel: note your count, then remove filler openings, doubled adjectives and sentences that repeat a point. Check keyword density last to catch words you leaned on too often.

    Using Flow Score to fix monotone writing

    Turn on Flow Score and look for runs of three or more sentences of similar length. Splitting one long sentence or joining two short ones usually makes the rhythm feel less mechanical.

    About us

    WordCounterPro is a small, independent writing tool built to do one thing well: show you the numbers behind your text while you type. It is free, has no accounts and shows no ads.

    Our approach

    Everything runs in your browser. Your writing is never uploaded, and the site is a single page of plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript hosted on GitHub Pages, so there is little to break and nothing hidden.

    What it can and can't do

    It counts, estimates and highlights. It does not check grammar, spelling or plagiarism, because those need outside services that would see your text.
