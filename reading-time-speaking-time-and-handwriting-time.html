How Long Will It Take? Reading, Speaking and Handwriting Time

If you’re timing a speech, a video script or a blog post, word count converts to minutes with a simple rule of thumb.

Typical speeds

  • Silent reading: around 238 words per minute for adults reading non-fiction, according to research that is widely used for reading-time estimates.
  • Speaking: often 130–170 words per minute in presentations. WordCounterPro uses 150.
  • Handwriting: much slower, commonly quoted at around 20 words per minute for copying text.

Working out the time

Divide your word count by the speed. A 1,500-word article read silently at 238 words per minute takes about six minutes and 18 seconds. Spoken at 150 words per minute, it takes ten minutes.

Make it fit you

You are not an average. Read a paragraph aloud with a timer, work out your own words per minute, and enter it in Settings so every estimate matches how you actually read, speak or write. Remember that pauses, slides, technical terms and difficult material all make real-world times longer than the estimate.

Using it well

For a talk, aim a little under your slot so you have room to breathe. For web content, reading time is a helpful hint to readers, but it should never replace good headings and a clear first paragraph.
