Avoiding Keyword Stuffing in Modern SEO

In the early days of search engine optimization, web creators believed repeating target keywords dozens of times would guarantee top rankings. Today, search algorithms penalize keyword stuffing heavily in favor of natural, high-value content.

What is Healthy Keyword Density?

As a general rule of thumb, keeping your primary keyword frequency between 1% and 2.5% of your total word count ensures search engines understand your topic without sounding repetitive to human readers.

Using Keyword Tools Wisely

Checking your top 10 keywords and phrases in real-time lets you spot overused terms before publishing. Focus on synonyms, related topical phrases, and answering user intent rather than robotic keyword repetition.
