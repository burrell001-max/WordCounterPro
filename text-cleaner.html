Remove unwanted formatting clutter from your text with one click.

How to Use the Text Cleaner

Text copied from PDFs, emails, web pages and old documents often arrives with extra spaces, stray tabs and big gaps between paragraphs. The Text Cleaner tidies that up in one click. It only changes whitespace and never alters your words, and it runs in your browser, so your text is not sent to our servers.

  1. Paste your text into the box above.
  2. Choose a button. Use Remove Extra Spaces, Remove Blank Lines, or Clean Everything to do both and more.
  3. Review the result. There is no undo button, so keep a copy of the original if you might need it.
  4. Click Copy Text to copy the cleaned text to your clipboard.

What Each Button Does

ButtonWhat it does
Remove Extra SpacesReplaces every run of spaces or tabs with a single space. For example, two spaces after a period become one, and a tab becomes one space. Line breaks are not touched.
Remove Blank LinesShrinks any run of blank lines down to a single blank line. Your paragraphs stay separated, but large gaps disappear. It does not delete the single blank line between paragraphs.
Clean EverythingDoes both of the above, then removes spaces at the start and end of every line, including indentation.

What it does not do

  • It doesn't join broken lines. If your text was copied from a PDF with a line break at the end of every line, those breaks remain inside paragraphs.
  • It may not catch every invisible character. Special characters such as non-breaking spaces in the middle of a line can survive cleaning.
  • It doesn't change your words. It doesn't fix spelling, grammar or punctuation.

When to Clean Your Text

  • After copying from a PDF or email. These sources often add spacing you can't see until you paste the text somewhere else.
  • Before submitting a form or application. Extra spaces can cause entries to look wrong or be rejected.
  • Before checking a character limit. Spaces count as characters on most platforms, so cleaning can lower your total. Paste the result into the main word counter, which shows characters with and without spaces.
  • Before importing text into another tool. Spreadsheets, databases and content systems handle clean text more reliably.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the Text Cleaner change my words?

No. It only changes spaces, tabs, blank lines and spacing at the edges of lines. Your words and punctuation stay the same.

Why are some of my line breaks still there?

The tool reduces runs of blank lines but doesn't remove line breaks inside a paragraph. If every line of your pasted text ends with a break, you'll need to join those lines yourself.

Will cleaning change my word count?

Your word count stays the same because no words change, but your character count may drop because extra spaces are removed.

Does it remove bold, italics or links?

The box holds plain text, so pasting into it already drops formatting such as bold, italics and links. Only the text itself remains.

Can I undo the cleaning?

There is no undo button on this page. Copy your original text before cleaning if you want to keep it.

Is my text uploaded or saved?

No. Cleaning happens in your browser, and this page doesn't send your text to our servers or save it.
